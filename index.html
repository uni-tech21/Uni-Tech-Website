A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Ask us anything ↗
What areas do you cover?+

We support homes and small businesses across Kent and East Sussex. Let us know your location and what you need so we can discuss suitable arrangements.

How much will it cost?+

Every device and project is different. Tell us what you need and we’ll discuss the assessment, available options and pricing before starting work.

Can you help if I’m not good with technology?+

Absolutely. We explain things in plain English, and our one-to-one tutoring sessions are tailored to your pace and what you want to learn.

Can you update an existing website?+

Yes, we can discuss updates and maintenance as well as new websites. Send us your current website address and the changes you have in mind.
